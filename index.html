"Un sirippil oru kavithai,

"Unnai varnikka vaarthaigal thevai illai,

In your voice flows sweetness, and in your presence, a rare happiness.

No words are needed to praise you, no reasons needed to call you special—

Just being you is enough; in this world, you are a beautiful blessing.
